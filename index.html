Trajectory Pool

A standalone billiards physics sandbox: elastic collisions, cushion bounces, and ghost-ball aim prediction.

Open table — pot a solid (1–7) or stripe (9–15) to claim your group.
Aim: drag on the felt to point the line. Power: drag the left slider, release to shoot (or press Space). Fine aim: drag the dial on the right rail for micro-adjustments. Reset: press R.
